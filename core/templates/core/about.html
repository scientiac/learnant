How Learnant works

  1. 01 / CREATE A WORKSPACE

    An institute starts its colony

    An institute administrator creates an organization workspace. New organizations begin with a 14-day trial.

  2. 02 / BUILD THE SYLLABUS

    Create courses and lessons

    Admins organize ordered lessons with Markdown, images, video, and mathematical notation.

  3. 03 / INVITE LEARNERS

    Enroll and assign members

    Tenant admins add learners individually or by CSV, then assign them to relevant courses.

  4. 04 / STUDY AND TRACK

    Learners follow their path

    Learners study assigned lessons, mark work complete, and see their own progress. Admins review progress for their colony.

Workspace roles

RoleScopeWhat they do
Super AdminPlatformOversees organizations and subscriptions, and can reactivate expired trials.
AdminPlatformManages and monitors organization workspaces.
Super ViewerPlatformRead-only platform-wide visibility.
Tenant AdminOne organizationAuthors courses, enrolls learners, assigns courses, and reviews progress.
Tenant UserOne organizationStudies assigned courses and tracks personal progress.

Production access: Learnant does not provide shared demo or evaluation accounts in production. Organization members are invited by their Tenant Admin; platform administrator accounts are provisioned by the platform operator.

Zero-Trust Isolation

Every query scopes by authenticated tenant. Foreign URL manipulation directly yields HTTP 404/403.

14-Day Free Trials

Self-signup initiates a 14-day trial. Expired colonies become read-only with non-destructive preservation.

Monochrome Sera

Minimalist shadcn/ui Sera aesthetic: sharp 0px corners, high typographic contrast, and native theme toggle.
